Questions before you book

Backup care, without the uncertainty.

How do I choose a campus?

Select directly from the full Chroma campus list and review the campus name and address before continuing.

Can I choose any Chroma campus?

Yes. The design supports all 24 campus choices without address lookup or location tracking.

What information will I need?

Expect basic parent and child details, emergency contacts, authorized pickup information, required acknowledgements, and any campus-specific arrival requirements.

When will I pay?

Payment belongs at the end of the secure booking flow, after the campus, date, age group, and required details are confirmed.
