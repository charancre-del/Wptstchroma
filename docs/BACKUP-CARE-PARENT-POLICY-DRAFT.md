# Backup Care Parent Policy Draft

Status: **Approval required before staging acceptance**
This is operational draft copy based on Chroma's approved business rules. It is not a substitute for legal, licensing, or insurance review.

## Parent-Facing Draft

### Eligibility and Enrollment

Backup Care is available to Chroma families and families who are not currently enrolled at Chroma. Campus approval is not required to reserve care. Each child must have a complete Backup Care enrollment record before payment, including emergency contacts, authorized pickup information, health information, medication or accommodation information, and required acknowledgments.

Children are eligible from **6 weeks old through 12 years old** on the care date. A child becomes ineligible on the 13th birthday.

Submitting health, medication, procedure, or accommodation information does not place the reservation into an approval queue. A paid booking remains confirmed. Chroma may contact the family before care to coordinate safe implementation of the submitted instructions.

### Price and Payment

Backup Care costs **$115 per child, per care date**. The price is charged for every selected child-date combination. GHL sends one invoice for the full booking and collects payment through the Stripe account connected inside GHL. A reservation is not confirmed until full payment succeeds and Chroma sends a confirmation.

### Booking Window and Arrival

Families may reserve care up to 365 days in advance. Care is available on eligible weekdays that are not marked closed. Same-day reservations must be completed by 7:30 AM and must provide at least 120 minutes of notice before the planned drop-off time. Every child must arrive by 9:30 AM. Families must pick up by the selected campus's published closing time.

### Campus Selection

The family chooses the campus for the entire order from the available Chroma campus list. Backup Care does not use browser location or Google address geocoding. The family remains responsible for confirming the selected campus name and address before requesting the invoice.

### Cancellation and Refunds

A paid child-date unit may be cancelled for a full **$115 refund** until 72 hours before that child's planned drop-off time. A cancellation made inside 72 hours is nonrefundable. The 72-hour rule has no discretionary exceptions. Cancelling one child or date does not cancel the remaining units in the order.

### Rescheduling

A paid child-date unit may be moved to another eligible date at the same campus until 72 hours before the original planned drop-off time. Rescheduling inside 72 hours is not allowed. The replacement date must satisfy the booking window, notice, weekday, closure, and capacity rules. The 72-hour rule has no discretionary exceptions.

### Chroma-Initiated Closures

Chroma will not accept new bookings for a date or campus marked closed.

If Chroma closes a campus after payment, the parent chooses either a full refund or one free reschedule for each affected child-date unit. Chroma does not make that choice automatically for the parent.

### Illness, Medication, and Accommodations

Parents must provide complete and accurate child information and comply with Chroma's illness-exclusion, medication, emergency, and authorized-pickup requirements. Chroma may refuse care at arrival when accepting the child would violate applicable health, safety, licensing, or staffing requirements. The payment treatment for a refused arrival must follow the final approved illness and safety policy.

### Communications and Records

The parent agrees to receive transactional booking, payment, cancellation, rescheduling, safety-coordination, and arrival communications. Chroma stores family and child enrollment information in GoHighLevel, collects payment through a GHL invoice using its connected Stripe account, and stores a limited encrypted transaction record in WordPress for booking fulfillment and audit purposes.
